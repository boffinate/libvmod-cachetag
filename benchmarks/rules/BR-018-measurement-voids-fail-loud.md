# BR-018: Measurement voids must fail loud; failed rows are diagnostic only

**Rule:** A repetition that produced no artifacts, matched no workloads, exited nonzero, materially under-sampled its configured interval, or failed a scope required by its frozen row definition is not eligible for the row's judged aggregate — never convert an overall-invalid repetition into a clean pass because useful stats happen to exist in its output; label the repetition diagnostic and retain the scope-specific validity reasons. Independently validated artifacts may remain valid in their own explicit scope, but they do not make the overall repetition valid and must not be included in the judged aggregate when another required scope failed.

Exact replacement is allowed when the failed required scope is attributable to measurement capture, such as under-sampling, missing process provenance, or a missing/truncated capture artifact. It is also allowed when the repetition completed the correct frozen work but BR-011 classified it `harness/under-saturated`, invalidating its required performance scope. A nonzero workload exit, wrong work volume, hit/error failure, candidate crash, driver-bound workload shape, or VMOD-attributed tail-budget failure is presumptively a behavioral or design failure, not a replaceable measurement failure. Do not replace it unless a documented root cause proves that the workload result itself was invalidated by an external harness failure. Do not discard already-valid repetitions when replacing an eligible invalid repetition. The judged row must still contain the minimum number of overall-valid repetitions required by BR-024 and must retain and identify every rejected repetition.

**Why:** A `BENCH_WORKLOAD_FILTER` matching no generated workload silently
exited 0 with no artifacts — a measurement void masquerading as success
(`devdocs/docs/archived/20260713_1615_diagnostic_phase4-stateless-origin-epoch.md`);
5M Fellow rows with functionally-useful driver data but watchdog/close failures were repeatedly at risk of being read as clean passes (`devdocs/docs/archived/fellow-xkey-memory-profiling-plan-2026-06-25.md`). During the Stream 1 campaign, a synchronous `smaps_rollup` read intermittently reduced a 0.1-second system sampler to four samples and missed the short-lived cache process. Re-running the entire three-repetition row discarded valid evidence and wasted substantial benchmark-host time without improving the failed capture.

**Comply by:** Checking the summarizer's overall-valid/invalid counts, scope reasons, sampler cadence, and required process provenance before reading any aggregate; keeping rejected/failed repetitions listed in campaign notes with reasons rather than deleting them. Freeze the required scopes for each row before examining candidate results. When exactly one planned repetition in the campaign is ineligible for its required judged scope for an eligible reason above, one and only one exact slot-for-slot replacement is authorized automatically and does not expand the BR-026 budget; a second ineligible repetition requires owner approval. A replacement repetition must preserve the frozen workload, implementation, harness measured path, host/power cohort, and ordering constraints. Use `CACHE_TAG_RUNS_OVERRIDE=1`, record the override in `remote-run.env`, and map the replacement to the rejected repetition. A replacement outside the original uninterrupted campaign command sequence must re-record host, power, runtime, and build provenance. If those remain unchanged, the single replacement may rely on the existing interleaved repetitions; a reboot, governor/boost change, benchmark-image rebuild, or other cohort-state change requires an adjacent control repetition selected by the frozen ordering. Record which condition applied. If candidate or measured-path harness identity changed, BR-017 requires a new cohort rather than treating the run as a replacement. BR-002 does not automatically require a new full A/A pair for every artifact replacement. Do not use performance values from a repetition that is invalid for the required judged scope. Record the accepted row as the original valid repetitions plus the explicitly mapped replacement; the rejected repetition remains diagnostic and is not extra evidence.
